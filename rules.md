PARADOX POWER — Rules v7 Usurpation • UDHR Protection on Institutions • Nullification Counters • Capture-All Victory 
1. GOAL Control all four Institutions (University, Corporate, Media/Culture, State Bureaucracy) with no UDHR protection coins remaining in any of their slots. When you do, you win — the state is captured and the remaining rights no longer function. A player may hold all four cards at once. That does not automatically win. Other players can still (a) protect those Institutions with UDHR coins, or (b) usurp the leading player and take the Institutions for themselves. 
2. INSTITUTIONS — CAPTURE, FREE, PROTECT All four Institution cards start in the centre of the table. When you capture one, place it in front of you. It belongs to you. No writing. Ownership is physical position only. • Capture — take the card from the centre (or from another player via Usurpation or card effect) and place it in front of you. • Free — return an Institution card to the centre. Any UDHR coins that were on it return to the shared UDHR supply. • Protect — place one or more UDHR coins into the protection slots printed on an Institution card (yours or an opponent’s, according to the effect used). 
3. 2.1 UDHR Protection Slots Each Institution card has three slots. Other players may place UDHR coins into those slots (by playing UDHR cards, spending a Restore action, or specific effects). While even one UDHR coin sits in a slot on any Institution you hold, that Institution is still under the protection of the Declaration. You cannot claim the total-capture win until every protection coin is gone from all four cards you hold. UDHR coins are limited. When the shared supply runs out, no further protection can be placed until coins return to the supply (by freeing an Institution or by effects that refund coins). 
4. 2.2 Nullifying Protection (Marxist Counters) The player who holds Institutions may nullify UDHR protection coins on cards they control if they have a valid counter. Counters include: • Playing a card that explicitly removes or ignores UDHR protection (e.g. certain Historical Purge, Ministry of Truth, or State Formation effects). • Spending 2 Grievance + discarding 1 card as a dedicated ‘Nullify Protection’ action (once per turn): remove 1 UDHR coin from an Institution you control and return it to the supply or discard it. • ESG/IMF Pressure or similar Corporate effects that force an opponent to remove a protection they just placed. Nullified coins leave the slot. When the last protection coin is removed from the last Institution you hold, and you hold all four, you may declare victory on your turn. 
5. USURPATION — TAKING CONTROL OF THE MARXIST PARTY Holding many Institutions makes you the visible leader of the revolutionary / institutional project — the de facto head of the ‘Marxist party’ at the table. Other players may attempt to usurp you: seize control of that position and take your Institution cards. 
6. 3.1 How to Usurp On your turn, as one action, if a single opponent holds 2 or more Institutions, you may declare an Usurpation attempt against them. Cost: 2 Grievance (or 1 Grievance + 1 card discard). Resolve as follows: 1. The target may spend 1 Power (Marxist Coin) or discard a Defense card to block the attempt. If they do, the Usurpation fails and your action is spent. 
7. If not blocked, you and the target each reveal the top card of the main Contradiction deck (or play one card from hand, table choice). Higher cost wins; on a tie the usurper wins. 
8. If you win the Usurpation: take all Institution cards the target currently holds and place them in front of you. Any UDHR protection coins on those cards stay on them (you inherit the protections). The target keeps their personal Marxist Coins and hand. 
9. If you lose: you lose 1 Power (or 1 Grievance if you have no Power). The target remains in control. Usurpation is the main way the table can stop a runaway leader without needing to free every Institution one by one. It models the historical pattern: the party eats its own leaders; control of the apparatus changes hands while the apparatus itself remains. 
10. 3.2 Team / Alliance Usurpation (optional) Two or more players may jointly pay the Usurpation cost and combine their revealed card costs against the target. If they succeed, they choose one among them to receive the Institution cards (or split them by agreement). 
11. WIN CONDITION (PRECISE) You win at the start of your turn (or immediately when the condition is met on your turn) if: • You hold all four Institution cards in front of you, and • There are zero UDHR protection coins remaining in the slots of those four cards. Optional stricter mode (recommended for longer games): the shared UDHR Integrity Track must also be at 0. Until both (or all three) conditions are true, other players can still protect, usurp, or free Institutions. State capture is not complete while the Declaration still has coins on the table or on the cards. 
12. WHAT OTHER PLAYERS CAN DO AGAINST A LEADER • Place UDHR coins into the protection slots on Institutions the leader holds (via UDHR cards or Restore actions). • Free an Institution (return it to the centre) with card effects. • Attempt Usurpation to take all the leader’s Institutions at once. • Drive the leader’s Power to 0 so they become Dependent and easier to strip. • Race to capture Institutions themselves and become the new leader. 
13. 5.1 What the Leader Can Do • Nullify UDHR protection coins on Institutions they control (counters / Nullify action). • Use ESG/IMF Pressure and purges to keep opponents weak and short of Grievance. • Hold Defense cards to block Usurpation attempts. • Finish clearing the last protection coins and claim victory. 
14. COMPONENTS REMINDER • Institution cards (v6) — centre → player area; slots for UDHR coins. • Marxist Coins — personal Power. • UDHR Coins — shared track + protection slots on Institutions. Finite supply. • Main Contradiction deck + UDHR Cards deck (Chance-style).
